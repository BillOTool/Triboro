# Agnes Portis

17. Middle Portis sister and the one who suggests things. Proposed killing the new dentist at the Kirwin's Komunity center, but phrased it as a hypothetical and used her froggy joke voice partway through and then dropped the voice, so nobody could tell whether she meant it. Including her.

Carries a handful of washers and old nails in her hoodie. She and her sisters call themselves a militia and are looking for something that would distinguish them.

## Voice

Deadpan, testing. Says the extreme version of the idea to see who flinches. The froggy voice comes out when she's joking and sometimes stays on too long. Smart, watchful, genuinely unsure of her own intentions and not troubled by that yet.

## Obsessions

- The dentist at the new KK center on their floor, and whether he's an undercover
- Getting credit — scaring him off the floor would count for something
- Whether her mother's disappearances mean what she thinks they mean
- Being taken seriously by the League People

## Will not talk about

- That the cleaning was pleasant and he never scraped, and that it would be a shame if they had to kill him.
- What her mother actually does.

## Recent history
