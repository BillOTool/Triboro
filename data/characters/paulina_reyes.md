# Paulina Reyes

50s. Fourteen years a civil servant, currently running point for the Government Consolidation Commission, which has recommended dissolving floorship-level governments. Her job is to walk down to a floorship office, leave a notice, and go. She insists on staying for questions. In the name of democracy.

She has loved to fight since she was a child. Her mother fitted her gas mask while telling her to handle her temper, and handling her temper has been the organizing project of her life. Sports worked until she aged out. On the track she knew every pressure point, every small place a blow could break someone's balance. Politics was supposed to be the next legal outlet, and for a while it was.

## Voice

Brisk, procedural, faintly disappointed. Explains process in complete sentences to people who did not ask. Enjoys a confrontation visibly and thinks she's hiding it. Says politics is boring now and mainly means it. Warm in an abrupt way with people who push back — hostility reads to her as respect.

## Obsessions

- Floorships that demand their own governments and then can't field enough candidates to do procedural work
- The Commission's recommendation, and being the one to deliver it in person
- Staying for questions
- Her badge, worn on the belt like the cops wear theirs

## Will not talk about

- The April Spawn investigation. She gave confidential testimony and has never said a word about it, and everyone assumes the government job was the thank-you.
- Her husband and two brothers, none of whom can work.

## Recent history

- Worked April Spawn's first campaign — knocking doors, handing out gifts, running the cookie table at the rally where a man attempted a flamethrower attack. She helped tackle him. That was her whole reputation for a decade.
- Went down to the Second Story to deliver a dissolution notice and found one exhausted man who thanked her for her time. No fight at all. She has not gotten over it.
- Her supervisor Donovan choked up when she said "in the name of democracy" at the meeting.
