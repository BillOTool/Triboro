# Walt Prentice

30s. Assigned to the new Kirwin's Komunity center on Floor 31. Claims to be a dentist by training, and by all available evidence is an extremely good one — cleans teeth without scraping, doesn't make small talk during the scrape, has the long tiny hands of a dentist rather than the thick mitts of a rent-a-cop.

It is widely assumed that the Komunity centers are cover for Kirwin's private detectives, sited where the zoning doesn't allow food storage and the League happens to be close. If Walt is an undercover, he is a very good one.
