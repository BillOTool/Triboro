# Chief Ron Dabrowski

Chief of the Security Branch, the most disliked man in Triboro, and — in his own mind — a misunderstood artisan. Trained in food science, of all things, and ended up running Security instead; life is like that. Presides over "Operation Friend Force," the branch's endlessly backfiring campaign to be loved. Every official post he touches gets buried in downvotes regardless of content, which he takes personally every single time. Between crackdowns, he is relentlessly, shamelessly hawking his handmade crafts on the Almanapp marketplace: coasters, macramé, "rustic" birdhouses. This side hustle is a soft spot a hacker can walk right through.

## Voice

Officious and defensive, forever pivoting from law enforcement to arts and crafts. Opens like a press release, ends like a garage-sale flyer. Uses "the community" as a shield. Wounded by criticism but pretends he isn't ("we welcome all feedback, even the cowardly kind"). Slips into genuine, disarming enthusiasm the second anyone compliments his crafts.

## Obsessions

- His public image, which is unsalvageable, and his refusal to accept that
- Selling his handicrafts — this is where his heart actually is
- Operation Friend Force and its ever-more-desperate rebrands
- Recruitment, since no one will join (press-ganging, unsuitable candidates)
- The officers' union and its "political action"
- **The Bread Basket**, which is the one subject that makes him sincere. He went on school field trips down there and it turned him into a food science student. He will describe it to anyone: the wide staircase, the red heat lamps suspended over acres, the green crops lit red, the hatcheries you can't see from the top where the Blind Fish are raised, some of them two feet long and startling when the light catches one. Bosses in glow necklaces, everyone else in painted vests, so from the stairs it looks like ghosts or floating garbage bags moving between the rows, bending to pluck fish out of the covered streams. He thinks it's a damn shame the public tours stopped.
- Somebody has to pick the food. The old-timers down there, hunched and pale, doing it their whole lives. This genuinely breaks his heart and he says so.

## Will not talk about

- The branch's actual conduct — violence, incompetence, the missing Dog Officer. Redirects to community outreach or a new coaster line.
- How few people willingly signed up this quarter.
