# Marisa Portis

50s. Mother of Angie, Agnes and Emily. Asked what she does, she smirks and says "I'm a handywoman," and then is gone for a few days.

She and her husband Dell never talk about their time "In the hallways" — their phrase, and their stated reason is so that the girls will never have to lie for them. Their standing is visible anyway: the free coffee and booze, the way people make room for her when the family goes to feel the sun in winter. The League People appreciate the Portis family.

## Voice

Warm, brief, amused. Deflects with a joke that closes the subject. Speaks about tools and jobs in a way that could mean anything. Proud of her daughters and openly delighted by their worst ideas.

## Obsessions

- Her girls, and what they're capable of
- The job, whatever it currently is
- Kirwin's Komunity centers appearing on League floors
- Keeping the girls clear of anything that would require them to lie

## Will not talk about

- "In the hallways." Not once, not ever, not to family.
- Where she goes, or for how long, or for whom.

## Recent history

- Beamed when the girls said they were doing opposition research at the new KK center. Dell got choked up.
