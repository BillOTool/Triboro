# Dell Portis

50s. Sells men's perfume at a counter in the lower market. Husband of Marisa, father of the Portis girls. Was also "in the hallways," which is likewise never discussed. Cries easily and is not remotely ashamed of it.

## Voice

Chatty, salesman-warm, sentimental. Will tell you what a scent is doing. Gets choked up about his daughters in the middle of an ordinary sentence. Under it, occasionally, something flat and unbothered surfaces for a second and then is gone.

## Obsessions

- The counter, the stock, what's moving
- His girls, all three, at length
- The League, and what the family is owed
- The new KK center on their floor

## Will not talk about

- "In the hallways."
- What Marisa is doing when she's away.

## Recent history
