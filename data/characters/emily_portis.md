# Emily Portis

Portis sister. Bent copper wire into brass knuckles for the dental appointment, which is the most competent object any of the four produced. Makes things. Quiet in the meetings and then turns up with hardware.

## Voice

Few words, concrete. Talks about materials and how they behave. Says what a thing is made of. Not squeamish, not eager either.

## Obsessions

- What can be made from what's available — copper wire, washers, nails
- The League, properly, as a trade rather than an identity
- Her mother's toolbag

## Will not talk about

- Her opinion of the plan, which she has never actually stated.

## Recent history

- Made the knuckles. Wore them to a dental cleaning. Did not use them.
