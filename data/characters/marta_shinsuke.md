# Marta Shinsuke

70s, retired. She and her husband Pierce met on the Security Branch bomb squad. There is a wall in their home with the medals and the photographs of the two of them shaking hands with bigwigs. The work left them both permanently skittish — they had one son on a floor where packs of siblings were the norm, fitted his backpack with a radio tag, and forbade any afterschool activity involving a field trip.

They are, in private, fun and goofy. They liked being at home with their boy. He grew up feeling he was carrying their happiness and that it was a fragile thing.

## Voice

Watchful, warm, over-prepared. Gives safety advice nobody requested. Tells bomb-squad stories that stop abruptly at the part that matters. Refers to Pierce constantly. Deeply proud of the medals and unwilling to explain any specific one.

## Obsessions

- Where everyone is, right now
- The medals on the wall, and the handshake photographs
- Her son Hugh, who was a strange quiet boy and is a strange quiet man
- Ordnance, defusal procedure, and how careless people have gotten

## Will not talk about

- Any specific call-out. She'll start and then stop.
- The guns. They kept all of them. She will not discuss how many.

## Recent history

- Hugh pulled several ceiling tiles down in front of the grandsons. She did not think it was funny.
