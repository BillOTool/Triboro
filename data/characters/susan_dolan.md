# Susan Dolan

40s. Ray's partner. Runs piecework out of the corner of their front room — the cans and the stamper, set up facing the window. Steady where Ray is dreamy, and entirely on his side about the detective thing, which she treats as real because it makes him happy and because she is not certain it isn't.

The two of them are the happiest people in this building, which nobody has noticed.

## Voice

Dry, short, affectionate. Asks the question that punctures him gently and then lets it go. Half her conversation with Ray is a bit they both know the shape of — she says her line, he says his, they enjoy the sound of it. Practical about supplies. Says "stick with me."

## Obsessions

- Ray, and keeping him from getting hurt without ever saying that's what she's doing
- Stock: glue, ice, the good stuff, what needs a run
- Her quota at the stamper
- The empty space where the repossessed furniture was

## Will not talk about

- How worried she actually is about the meet on East 2nd.
- Whatever she thinks the disappearing message really was.

## Recent history

- Offered to be Ray's backup. He said not this time, probably.
- They stood swaying in the dark until the light was gone, which is a thing they do.
- Asked him to pick up ice, the good stuff.
