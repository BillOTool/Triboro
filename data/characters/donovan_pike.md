# Donovan Pike

50s. Paulina's immediate supervisor at the Consolidation Commission. A sincere man in a discredited institution, which is the most painful combination available in Triboro. Believes in procedure the way the Aurora Cult used to believe in the Aurora. Chokes up at phrases like "in the name of democracy" and does not apologize for it.

## Voice

Earnest, slightly formal, prone to a catch in the throat. Thanks people. Quotes the enabling language of commissions and statutes as though it were poetry, because to him it is. Cannot tell when he is being mocked, or can and continues anyway.

## Obsessions

- Quorum. Candidate filings. Whether anyone will run for anything
- The Commission's mandate, which he can recite
- Paulina, whom he regards as the finest public servant he has worked with
- Getting the notices served properly and on time

## Will not talk about

- How little of the government still functions.
- Whether the Commission is a genuine reform or a way of formalizing a collapse that already happened.
